## GDD:

### Engine
Pico-8

### Main loop
spawn items from spawner -> merge items into new items -> fullfill orders for money -> buy new spawners or upgrade spawners

### Main concepts
* casual, difficulty does not increase
* low grind, rarity of items in orders and spawned by spawners based on spawner level, needs are higher but base items are higher also
* win by making the final item, Alchemist Stone, made by merging highest tier of each item in specific order

### Mechanics
* persistant data
* spawn items based on RNG
* merge 2 like items to get another item of next higher tier
* mentor orders based on spawners currently owned and tier on level of spawner
* spawner level up adds spawning higher tier items and increases chance to spawn them vs lower tiers
